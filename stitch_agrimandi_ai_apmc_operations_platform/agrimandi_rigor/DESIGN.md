## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an enterprise-grade, high-integrity digital ledger environment engineered specifically for agricultural commission agents (Adhatiyas) within Indian APMC (Agricultural Produce Market Committee) mandis. Operating at the chaotic convergence of fast-moving physical auctions, perishable produce weights, variable grading, and massive daily credit lines, the interface demands uncompromising precision, zero cognitive friction, and absolute trustworthiness.

The design philosophy combines **Corporate / Modern** utilitarian discipline with high-density data display. It eschews decorative marketplace tropes, consumer gradients, and skeuomorphic agricultural motifs in favor of surgical information density, systematic line-item auditing, and high-visibility financial clarity. The visual emotional tone is authoritative, sober, resilient, and audit-ready—mirroring the dependability of traditional ledger books (*bahi-khata*) while accelerating daily workflows across auction floor settlement, farmer payout disbursement, buyer invoicing, and returnable packaging asset tracking (crates, gunny bags).

## Colors

The color palette centers on a disciplined forest green primary that honors agricultural prosperity without sacrificing institutional financial gravity. Surface structures rely on neutral slates, crisp boundary rules, and deliberate semantic accents designed for instantaneous state triage on demanding operational dashboards.

### Primary & Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#134E35`):** Reserved for institutional anchor elements, primary action triggers, primary table selections, and core system navigation bars.
- **Primary Accent / Hover (`#1B6344`):** Secondary interactive states, pressed tab markers, and elevated primary buttons.
- **Informative Cobalt (`#2563EB`):** Auction stream status indicators, lot tracking telemetry, and verified ledger metadata links.

### Canvas & Structural Neutrals
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** Application canvas background for low-glare outdoor/sunlit tablet and desktop terminal visibility.
- **Canvas Muted Substrate (`#F1F5F9`):** Grouping wrappers, nested ledger summary cards, and frozen table headers.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Workstation cards, data grids, modal dialogs, and popovers.
- **Border / Structural Grid (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise 1px borders delineating cells, tabular records, and asset balance splits.
- **Focus / Ring Boundary (`#CBD5E1`):** Keyboard accessibility highlights and active input borders.

### Typographic Neutrals
- **Headings & Financial Primaries (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate-black providing crisp contrast for currency values and lot weights.
- **Body & Tabular Data (`#334155`):** Standard reading level for farmer/trader names, batch manifests, and notes.
- **Muted Metadata (`#64748B`):** Supporting units (Qtl, Kg, Crates), timestamps, GSTIN strings, and inactive column handles.

### Status & Balance Semantics
- **Settled / Surplus Green (`#16A34A`):** Instant clear balance, direct DBTs executed, weighbridge variance 0%.
- **Caution / Outstanding Amber (`#D97706`):** Partial payment released, weighment pending, unreturned packaging over 7 days.
- **Critical / Overdue Crimson (`#DC2626`):** Bad debts, auction payment dishonored, missing gunny bag asset penalty.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is divided purposefully between structural operational anchors (**Plus Jakarta Sans**) and intense tabular calculations (**Inter**).

### Financial & Numeral Rules
All currency displays, rates per quintal, bag counts, tare deductions, and transaction balances must enforce font-feature-settings: `"tnum"` (tabular figures) and `"cv05"` to guarantee vertical decimal alignment across row comparisons. Currency values always prefix the Indian Rupee symbol with explicit spacing: `₹ 1,42,850.00`.

### Readability Specifications
Table column headers enforce `label-sm` with upper-case transformation, subtle tracking (`0.04em`), and `#64748B` tint. This maximizes the scanability of wide multi-column ledgers (Lot ID, Farmer, Commodity, Bags, Gross Wt, Tare, Net Wt, Bid Rate, Cess, Net Payable).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an enterprise-grade 12-column responsive fluid grid configured for data density and screen real estate efficiency. Commission agents operate on wide desktop setups in back offices and high-durability rugged tablets at the auction pit.

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column layout with 24px (`space-lg`) gutters and 32px (`space-xl`) outer margins. Tables default to sticky summary side-panels or 70/30 split screens showing the live auction lane alongside active lot ledger details.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`gutter`) gutters and 24px outer margins. Multi-column tables collapse secondary metadata into expandable accordion drawers, preserving Gross, Net, Rate, and Action visibly pinned.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with 12px gutters and 16px margins. Primary workflows switch from dense data tables to high-contrast execution cards with full-width primary touch targets.

### Density Scales
Internal component padding follows a strict 4px grid baseline. Tabular rows utilize a compact vertical rhythm (`0.5rem` top/bottom padding) to ensure at least 15 visible transactional rows without scrolling on standard 1080p displays.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating cloud shadows, translucent blurs, or game-like depth cues, adopting **low-contrast outlines** paired with disciplined, **functional micro-elevation**. Mandi environments require sharp perimeter contrast under fluctuating ambient lighting.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Main background `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, zero drop shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card / Ledger Container):** `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. No shadow in resting state. Delineates work panels, summary metric blocks, and tables.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus / Row Select / Dropdown):** `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border and an ambient micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Weighbridge Alert Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border and a structured enterprise shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 4 (Sticky Docked Financial Actions):** Bottom ledger settlement bar fixed across viewports, bordered by a top 1px `#E2E8F0` rule and backed by `#FFFFFF` with `0 -4px 12px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** primitives. Crisp corners with micro-radii emphasize architectural stability, mechanical efficiency, and spreadsheet-level exactness.

- **Base Components (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form fields, inputs, standard buttons, tabular status badges, and chip filters.
- **Containers & Panels (`0.375rem` / 6px to `0.5rem` / 8px):** Structural cards, modal frames, and ledger panels.
- **Strictly Square (`0px`):** Table grid cells, sticky header bars, vertical data splitters, and frozen columns to maintain uninterrupted line continuity across dense grids.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Strictly reserved for dynamic auction live-status counters (e.g., "LIVE BIDDING", "WEIGHING") to visually separate ephemeral states from transactional records.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Confirm Sale / Disburse DBT):** Solid `#134E35` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` corner radius, `font-weight: 600`, zero border. Focus state features a 2px offset outline of `#1B6344`.
- **Secondary Action (Print Slip / Export Bahi-Khata):** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive (Void Lot / Cancel Invoice):** Crisp `#DC2626` text on `#FEF2F2` background with a 1px `#FCA5A5` stroke.

### Data Tables (The Mandi Core Grid)
- **Header Cells:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `#64748B` typography, 1px solid bottom border in `#CBD5E1`. Vertical height strictly `36px`.
- **Data Cells:** Background `#FFFFFF`, `#334155` text, 1px bottom border `#E2E8F0`. Height `44px` (dense mode) or `52px` (standard mode). Numeric cells right-aligned with `tnum` font features.
- **Row States:** Hover triggers `#F8FAFC`. Selected/checked auction row transitions to `#F0FDF4` with a 2px vertical left accent in `#134E35`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Settlement Status:** Subtle tint backgrounds with high-contrast foreground text:
  - *Paid:* `#DCFCE7` background, `#15803D` text, 1px `#BBF7D0` border.
  - *Pending / Unsettled:* `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text, 1px `#FDE68A` border.
  - *Defaulted / Overdue:* `#FEE2E2` background, `#B91C1C` text, 1px `#FECACA` border.
- **Commodity Variety Chips:** Compact, `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `font-size: 0.75rem`.

### Form Inputs & Numeric Steppers
- **Standard Field:** White background, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Inner padding `8px 12px`.
- **Focused State:** 1px solid `#134E35` border accompanied by an exact 1px box-shadow ring `#134E35`.
- **Weighbridge / Bid Inputs:** Integrated prefix/suffix modules (`₹` on left, `/ Qtl` or `Kg` on right) shaded in `#F1F5F9` with matching 1px border framing.

### Specialized Operational Modules
- **Returnable Packaging Asset Tracker:** Split inventory counter showing Issued, Returned, and In-Transit crates/bags. Red inline tag triggers if retention exceeds Mandi by-law thresholds.
- **Farmer Payout Reconciliation Bar:** Sticky footed ledger drawer summarizing Total Gross, Market Cess (1%), Commission (Adhat - 2.5%), Weighment Fee (Tol), and Net Payable to Bank Account via instant IMPS/NEFT with one-click verification.
